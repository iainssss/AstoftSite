Plaza de Mayo


                           

Casa Rosada (Pink House) or Casa de Gobierno, the presidential palace. There are two theories on the origin of the colour: That it was a peace-making attempt by President Sarmiento during his term 1868-74 to blend the red of the Federalists with the white of the Unitarists. Alternatively that it came from painting the palace with bovine blood, a common practice then.
