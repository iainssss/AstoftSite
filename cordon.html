The village of Cordon lies above the town of Sallanches and is often referred to as "The balcony of Mont Blanc" and has a superb 360� panorama of mountains.



The church of Cordon, L'Eglise Notre-Dame de l'Assomption, was built 1781-7. The shiny onion-domed spire dates from 1816. The church is a prime example of the Savoyard baroque style.



Inside, a highly decorated altar-piece with twisted columns. The nave, chancel and transepts have fine vaulted and painted roofs. The crossing has a cupola supported by four piers with pilasters . The spaces above the piers and the cupola are also richly painted, the latter in a trompe l�oeil architectural motif.
