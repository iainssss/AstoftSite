Highclere,  Hampshire - St Michael and All Angels Church
19th century

Click on photos to enlarge.
Notes in italics from Hampshire and the Isle of Wight by Nikolaus Pevsner and David Lloyd (1967)
Yale University Press, New Haven and London.



                       


1869-70 by Sir George Gilbert Scott. Flint and stone with a NE steeple carrying a broach-spire. Lancets and plate tracery. Low and dark interior with a S arcade. The chancel has a rose window in the E wall and shafted N and S windows, the shafts being detached. The altar composition seems entirely part of it, but dates from 1894.
