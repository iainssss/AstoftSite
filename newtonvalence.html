Newton Valence, Hampshire  -  St Mary Church
13th century

Click photos to enlarge.
Notes in italics from Hampshire and the Isle of Wight by Nikolaus Pevsner and David Lloyd (1967)
Yale University Press, New Haven and London.



                                       

The W tower is Early English (one small W lancet), but has a Perp W doorway (c.15th century) and a brick top of 1812. Nave and chancel look Victorian (restoration 1871), but are E.E., cf. the S lancet and in the chancel more lancets ... the E window of three stepped lancets very close together and under one arch ...


Interior views looking east and west. Wide N chapel of c.1300 attached to the nave.
