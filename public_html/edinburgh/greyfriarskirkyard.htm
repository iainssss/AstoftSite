Notes in italics from Pevsner Architectural Guides,
Edinburgh by John Gifford, Colin McWilliam and David Walker (1991), Yale University Press.

Greyfriars churchyard boasts the best collection of C17 monuments in Scotland. ...


Eastern boundary of the churchyard

                        

At the bottom of the eastern boundary the Martyrs' Monument commemorating the Covenanters. The present stone dates from 1771, replacing an earlier one of 1707 now in the Huntley House Museum.

              

View from the churchyard to the north and the steeple of the old Highland Tolbooth Church, now The Hub, at the top of the Royal Mile.
The western boundary and George Heriot's School through the Flodden Wall.

              

On the western boundary the tomb of Robert Adam's father, William Adam - Mausoleum of William Adam, died 1748, by John Adam, 1753. Round-arched entrance at the E, thermal openings at N and S formed by later infilling under the arches. Entablature with rams' skulls and rosettes between the triglyphs. Dome inside, carried on a circular cornice. Large inscription panels, the principal one at the W with a portrait bust of William Adam and a tomb-chest, its front carved with Hopetoun House with ruined portico. ...

Full list of works by the Adam family. Robert Adam Page. Robert Adam is buried in Westminster Abbey.
