Spinnaker Tower, Portsmouth
and Views of Portsmouth Harbour

  The Spinnaker Tower at Gunwharf Quays in Portsmouth was opened in 2005. It stands about 170 metres above Portsmouth Harbour and the Solent. The shape is based on a type of sail called a spinnaker (Wikipedia). There three viewing platforms at the top of the tower. The top platform, the Crow's Nest, is open to the air with a wire mesh roof . The glass floor on the bottom platform is the largest in Europe.
